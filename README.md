# PicklePark — Washington Park Pickleball Companion (Burlingame, CA)

**PicklePark** is the free iOS community companion app for pickleball players at Washington Park (850 Burlingame Ave, Burlingame, CA).

## Key Features
- **Live Court Dashboard:** Real-time court occupancy across Court 1 (4 permanent Open Play courts) and Courts 2 & 3 (Reservable / Walk-On courts).
- **Real-Time Est. Wait Time & Hourly Analytics:** Live wait-time math (triggers once all 4 courts are full using a 15-minute average game cycle) and hourly historical wait-time graphs built from real player reports.
- **Washington Park Lost & Found Board:** Scrollable community Lost & Found feed with `LOST` and `MARKED FOUND` badges, timestamps, item descriptions, and on-device Vision OCR safety moderation.
- **PickleMatch Doubles Rotations:** Built-in round-robin doubles generator and live score tracker.
- **Live Court Weather & EPA AQI:** Real-time wind, temperature, and air quality for Washington Park.
