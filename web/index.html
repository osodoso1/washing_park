Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

PicklePark is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe in providing simple, community-driven tools with zero invasive tracking.

1. Information We Collect

PicklePark does not require account creation, passwords, phone numbers, or email addresses to use the app. Any display name, match score, or leaderboard point you choose to input is stored locally on your device or submitted anonymously to community counters.

2. No Third-Party Tracking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or location brokers.

3. Local Device Storage

Tournament rosters, doubles rotation lineups, and profile preferences are stored locally on your device using standard Apple iOS storage and web local storage.

4. Contact Support

For questions or support regarding PicklePark, visit washingtonpb.pages.dev or use the in-app feedback tool.
